Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can you fix my phone?

    Most likely, yes. Phone screens, batteries, charging ports, and back panels are all fairly common repairs that go well around 95% of the time. If the phone is entirely unresponsive or has any water exposure then it may be worth considering a replacement.

  • Can you fix stuff other than phones?

    Yes! Pretty much as long as we can find the parts we can fix your device. Most electronics that will fit on a desk are repairable.

  • How does a technician visit work?

    A technician will show up in a work van and contact you by phone. You hand them the broken device and they'll get right to work. Feel free to watch and chat if you have the time. Once its repaired the tech will test it, confirm functionality with you, and be on their way.

  • Do you have a warranty?

    We offer a 1 year limited warranty on our parts and labor. This does not cover physical damage. As an example if you repair a phone screen but it randomly has green lines: that is under warranty. However if it has lines and a crack in one corner: that is not under warranty. Instead that would be covered under our Protection Plan.

  • When do you need payment

    Once you submit the "Repair my Device" form, we'll discuss the repair, and then you'll get a quote. Full payment is required at that time. This lets us provide the best service we can by covering parts and securing your scheduled repair.

  • What if you can't fix my device?

    If your device cannot be fixed despite our best efforts then we'll do what we can to at least save the data on the device. If it can be backed up or migrated to a different device then we can move forward with that goal. If nothing can be done then we'll recover and refund the replacement parts as well as the Protection Plan and $50 from the Base Price.
